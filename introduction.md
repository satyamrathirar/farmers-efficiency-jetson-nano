# 1. Introduction

The global population surge has intensified the demand for agricultural productivity, necessitating innovative approaches to address the challenges of sustainable farming practices. Traditional agricultural methods face significant limitations in meeting the growing food security requirements while maintaining environmental sustainability [1]. Modern agriculture confronts multifaceted challenges including weed management, crop monitoring, and precise navigation of agricultural machinery, all of which demand advanced technological solutions to optimize farming efficiency and reduce operational costs [2].

The development of intelligent systems to enhance agricultural productivity has emerged as a critical research domain across multiple disciplines. Among the most transformative innovations for modern farming are autonomous agricultural systems that leverage computer vision and artificial intelligence technologies. To achieve optimal crop management and field operations, farmers require sophisticated assistance systems capable of real-time decision-making and precise environmental perception. Extensive research and technological solutions have been developed to improve agricultural efficiency through automated weed detection, crop monitoring, and autonomous vehicle navigation systems [3].

These technological advancements form the foundation of precision agriculture systems and smart farming implementations. Precision agriculture systems are designed to assist farmers and agricultural machinery in identifying crop-related challenges and responding with accurate, timely interventions [4]. The proliferation of computer vision and deep learning technologies in the agricultural sector has led to increased focus on developing cost-effective, real-time monitoring and control systems. These systems have evolved into pivotal research areas due to their capacity to revolutionize traditional farming practices and significantly enhance agricultural productivity [5].

While various sensing technologies contribute to precision agriculture systems [6], camera-based solutions offer substantial advantages in terms of cost-effectiveness, versatility, and compatibility with state-of-the-art computer vision algorithms [7]. Agricultural environment perception involves the collection and analysis of comprehensive field information, encompassing crop health assessment, weed identification, soil condition monitoring, and navigation path planning [8]. The design of such systems must account for diverse environmental factors, including weather variations, lighting conditions, and field terrain characteristics, as these elements significantly impact the accuracy and reliability of vision-based agricultural monitoring systems [9].

Over the past decade, there has been substantial emphasis on developing intelligent agricultural monitoring systems aimed at enhancing farming efficiency through real-time field surveillance and automated decision-making capabilities. The integration of multiple sensing modalities, including RGB cameras, multispectral imaging, and LiDAR sensors, combined with advances in edge computing and GPU acceleration, has enabled researchers to create increasingly sophisticated solutions for real-time computer vision-based agricultural assistance systems [10]. Significant progress in agricultural sensor technology and embedded computing platforms has been documented, facilitating the implementation of various complex tasks including weed detection [11], crop classification [12], field navigation [13], and yield prediction [14] across diverse agricultural applications.

This research presents a comprehensive system for enhancing farming efficiency through the integration of deep learning-based weed detection and computer vision-based lane detection technologies, implemented on edge computing hardware for real-time agricultural field operations. The proposed system combines YOLOv4 and YOLOv5 architectures for accurate weed identification with Hough Transform-based lane detection for autonomous agricultural vehicle navigation, optimized for deployment on resource-constrained Jetson Nano devices to ensure practical field applicability and cost-effectiveness.
